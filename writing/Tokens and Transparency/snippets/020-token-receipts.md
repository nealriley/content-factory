There are a few reasons why you should be putting your token counts in front of users when you are selling through the consumption, metered, token model. One is just the trust that you are taking money from someones hand while you are also giving them immediate value. That representation of the transaction helps solidfy that there was value delivered at the point where cost is meant to be incurred. It is also this feedback mechanism which helps growing or power users learn how to adapt their use of the tool to optimise cost. INt his way, the responsibility for reducing cost overall is the vendor and the customer together. Shared risk shared reward.
