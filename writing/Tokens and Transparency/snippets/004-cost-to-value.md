There are times where token-based billing, or other types of metered pricing models make the most sense. Typically this is when consumption of the feature is something that is predictable, and consumer choice between different metered pricing tiers. For OpenAI's platform, there are a plethora of models available through a consistent interface, each with their own price and unique value. The ratio - cost to value, doesn't simply produce high-value goods, high value-per-cost is all about maximizing margin while maintaining an 'acceptable' output.
