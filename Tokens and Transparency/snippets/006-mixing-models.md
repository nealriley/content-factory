Vendors with smart commercial models often have monetization strategies that combine multiple pricing models together to help hedge this risk while providing some predictability and stability in the pricing provided to the end-customer. An example of this kind of blend is a commercial strategy that encourages activation and trial of the product through a 'freemium' model - capping the free usage both in quantity and breadth of features. This approach can use mutliple pricing models combined together: a free/evaluation period that is token-based with a maximum usage cap, and a more predictable subscription model for those paying customers.
