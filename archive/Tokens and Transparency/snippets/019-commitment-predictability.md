Predictability is built on some level of commitment. This can be from the end user in the form of minimum spend or pre-paying for a year’s worth of access. AI features and the specific hardware they require to operate benefit greatly from this sort of commitment, as they correspond with the physical server needed to bring on more compute capacity. Committed spend, especially in the world of B2B, also puts the customer relationship into one where the customer tries to find value as the cost is sunk. In healthy relationships with customers this can take the form of active investigation to getting good value for money. In toxic relationships this can take the form of a customer’s insistence that the product show its worth. Typically this happens through customer success or other relationship and account management.
