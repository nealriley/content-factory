When your pricing is static, for instance in cases where you are selling access by user rather than through metered billing, your only competition becomes changes to your product or the overall experience. In the case of a metered billing, the vendor has a lot more flexibility. This includes competing on price, as you can activate customers who will pay less than the standard rates charged by a user-based billing model. There will also naturally be times when the user-based approach will be advantageous pricing-wise over metered. Having a lower cost option that a user can switch between to meet that optimization gap can help. (VoidWriter)
