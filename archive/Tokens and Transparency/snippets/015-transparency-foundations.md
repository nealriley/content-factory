The problem with transparency in modern digital products is that the problem is simply difficult. The enhanced penalties of exposing anything that looks like customer information, whether it qualifies explicitly as PII or not, and the penalties that come with that notwithstanding. Transparency requires two main foundations: you have to define and design the units of measure of your product, and you have to find ways to easily expose the right view of this information to your end users. This will change over time, and in the case of team-based applications it depends on the roles of the people in the team. This requires understanding that right level of information, as well as baking into the product as a whole to ensure that information about the use of the product can flow back to the user.
