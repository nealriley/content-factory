When dealing with a non-metered pricing model, the onus is on the vendor to adjust overall margin by fluctuating costs. The costs of running LLMs are flexible, as there are a plethora of different value-added service providers, as well as a set of standards that makes interoperability at a systems level possible. Investing time in adopting lower cost infrastructure to meet a common and predictable need is a very useful strategy for reducing this cost per user, but it is a distraction from the R and D necessary to adopt new flagship models as they enter the market. The tradeoff becomes one of optimizing today’s value vs building for tomorrow’s interaction model. The answer for every business is always a pragmatic answer both, but the balance is difficult to strike and is a shifting landscape. (VoidWriter)
