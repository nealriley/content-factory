# Outline: Clear Meters after Elena’s Credits Take

- Hook: Credits as a stopgap, not a destiny — what changes when the meter goes visible?
- What Elena nailed: credits tame runaway LLM costs, but opacity poisons trust.
- Show the meter, shrink the burn: Lovable’s 20–30% credit cuts by tightening prompts and workflows.
- Anchor + meter: base subscription or seat floor plus tokens for variable-heavy features.
- Prepay without fear: token packs with rollover/quarterly expiry to budget spiky workloads.
- Commit to consume: annual token commitments with quarterly true-ups to earn predictability discounts.
- Let buyers self-forecast: define the unit, show real-time balance, publish the math on invoices.
- Choice beats lock-in: let customers switch between user-based and metered models to fit their pattern.
- Reward efficiency, don’t punish growth: lower burn for optimized workflows or off-peak use.
- Fairness and comparison: standardize what a “credit” is so customers can benchmark value across vendors.
- Predictability in a volatile AI era: balance today’s cost optimizations with tomorrow’s model upgrades.
- Close: transparent meters plus predictable anchors win trust until per-user pricing returns.
