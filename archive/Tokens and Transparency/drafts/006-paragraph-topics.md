# Paragraph Topics: Tokens and Transparency

Brief:
- Goal: catalog 20 paragraph ideas with one-line intros to guide the next round of drafts.
- Audience: product and finance leads evaluating hybrid token + subscription pricing.
- Promise: rapid menu of angles that keep transparency and predictability front and center.
- Sources: Elena Verna’s “My beef with AI credit pricing” plus internal snippets in this project.

1. Why tokens need anchors: Show how a visible meter only works when paired with a predictable base fee.
2. Seats vs. usage: Contrast collaboration seats with tokenized execution so both roles understand the split.
3. Prepaid packs with rollover: Explain quarterly-expiring token packs that calm finance teams without hiding the meter.
4. Commit-to-consume: Introduce annual token commitments with quarterly true-ups to trade predictability for discount.
5. Transparency on invoices: Describe how publishing the meter math on every invoice builds trust.
6. In-product balance visibility: Make the case for showing real-time token balance and projected burn inside the app.
7. Efficiency incentives: Outline how lowering burn for optimized workflows rewards the right behavior.
8. Off-peak pricing: Position off-peak token discounts as a fairness lever, not a gotcha.
9. Defining the unit: Argue for a standard “credit” definition so buyers can benchmark across vendors.
10. Apples-to-apples comparisons: Show why opaque credits block buyers from comparing price-performance.
11. Risk of exploitation: Flag how hidden meters invite gaming and erode trust in the model.
12. Handling spiky workloads: Explain hybrid models that absorb seasonal spikes without punitive overages.
13. Forecasting tools: Pitch self-service forecasting so buyers can model burn before signing.
14. Procurement alignment: Detail the talking points that reassure procurement on predictability and governance.
15. Margin protection: Show how anchors plus visibility protect vendor margins while customers optimize.
16. Customer success workflows: Describe CS playbooks that review burn, tune prompts, and prevent surprise invoices.
17. Model upgrades and volatility: Note how transparent meters help manage changes when models evolve.
18. Switching between models: Suggest letting customers toggle between seat-heavy and usage-heavy plans.
19. Fairness narrative: Frame transparency as a fairness promise that keeps long-term relationships healthy.
20. CTA and next steps: Close with the invitation to publish meters, set anchors, and pilot transparent usage now.
